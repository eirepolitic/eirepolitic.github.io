# Revenue Investigation — Wave 3 Stage 2 Viability Filter

## Decision summary

Wave 3 was filtered more aggressively than the earlier waves. Many ideas had clear user pain but current specialist products already solve the exact workflow cheaply.

## Evidence that materially changed the filter

- Help-centre gap detection is already a native feature of current support platforms such as Help.center.
- Contractor onboarding/document chasing is already available from low-cost tools such as SubCada, TrackMyVendor and CertBunker.
- Commercial lease abstraction is a crowded 2026 category with published pricing from single-digit/per-lease tools through enterprise suites.
- Event sponsor asset collection is now served by multiple purpose-built tools including SponsorAssetHub, SponsorDrop, GatherMonk, SponsorHQ and TierBook.
- Supplier price-list change detection is already sold by focused products such as PriceListIQ at roughly US$39/month and broader procurement/pricing platforms.
- Technician photo-to-report workflows are heavily served by field-service products such as FieldSign, JobTrail, CapProof, FieldEx and others.
- HVAC warranty registration now has dedicated tools such as Veribl as well as manufacturer-specific contractor apps.
- HubSpot stale-deal detection can be handled with native workflows/scoring and now has third-party tools specifically targeting the same gap.
- Supplier specification comparison/change intelligence is directly served by tools such as SpecMake and Motifuse.
- COA generation/management is a mature quality/compliance workflow with specialist products such as Worldover and broader traceability systems.
- Wholesale reorder portals are a mature category with self-serve products beginning around tens of dollars per month.

## Stage 3 shortlist

Only two ideas justify deep investigation:

1. **W3-I1 — recurring mystery-enquiry/lead-response monitoring**, specifically for agencies or multi-location businesses rather than individual one-off audits.
2. **W3-G5 — EDI-lite order translation**, specifically for smaller suppliers receiving structured CSV/Excel/email purchase orders that are not worth a full EDI integration.

A third concept is retained only as a possible variant rather than a separate Stage 3 candidate:

- **W3-G3 + W3-L3 — supplier price-change + margin-impact report** for small distributors. Current competitors prove demand, but the generic product is already commoditized; only a narrower vertical/service angle would justify revival.
