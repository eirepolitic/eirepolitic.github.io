# Revenue Investigation — Wave 2 Stage 2 Viability Filter

## Method

Same six 1–5 estimates used in Wave 1: startup cost, speed to first revenue, low ongoing effort, automation potential, low legal/platform risk and upside. Higher is better. Scores are screening estimates, not validated economics.

## Evidence that changed the filter

- **Shopify CSV validation is already free and crowded.** Several current tools provide browser-based Shopify product CSV preflight checking. Sources: https://tools.tnaado.ca/creator/shopify-product-csv-preflight/ and https://activory.com/tools/shopify-csv-validator
- **QuickBooks import validation already has a simple paid incumbent** at roughly US$15/year. Source: https://www.importvalidator.com/
- **Shopify returns analytics is increasingly native/commoditized.** Shopify documents return analytics and specialized apps already analyze SKU-level return cost/reasons. Sources: https://www.shopify.com/blog/returns-management-software and https://apps.shopify.com/returns-intelligence-1
- **Agency form monitoring has multiple direct competitors.** FormHeartbeat, LeadUptime and FormTracker already monitor live form paths. Sources: https://formheartbeat.com/ , https://leaduptime.com/ , https://www.formtracker.io/solutions/marketing-agency-form-monitoring
- **Supplier/freight quote comparison is crowded.** Current products include QuoteCompare, VendorIQ, Rapido and free AI comparison tools. Sources: https://getquotecompare.com/ , https://vendoriq.pl/en/supplier-quote-comparison/ , https://rapidohub.com/freight-quote-comparison-software/ , https://www.meshworks.com/free-tools/quote-analysis
- **Generic affiliate link monitoring has specialist competitors.** Sources: https://verflink.com/affiliate-link-checker and https://clickolytics.com/learn/comparisons/best-broken-link-checkers
- **Canadian contract-award intelligence is already crowded.** Current products/search layers include Ottawasted, ContractFinder, Wonable, ProposalForge and an Apify award-data Actor. Sources: https://ottawasted.ca/contracts/awards , https://www.contractfinder.ca/award-intelligence , https://wonable.io/awards , https://proposalforge.io/canadian-awards , https://apify.com/zhucl1006/canadabuys-contract-awards
- **Generic synthetic-data generation is free and plentiful.** Examples include Postman and several browser-only generators. Sources: https://www.postman.com/tools/test-data-generator and https://www.data-generator.io/
- **RFP compliance extraction has demonstrated paid demand.** RFP Matrix currently lists US$100 for a single RFP and subscription tiers beginning around US$150/month. Source: https://rfpmatrix.com/
- **Sponsor reporting is a visible manual pain point.** Current products and publisher guidance explicitly address sponsor-ready reporting, while creator discussions describe manual screenshot/report assembly. Sources: https://bannertrackr.com/blog/newsletter-sponsor-report-template , https://podseo.com/features/proof-and-reporting/ and Reddit discussion captured during verification.
- **QR equipment/manual access is proven but mature.** Current facility products provide QR access to manuals, warranties and service history. Sources: https://www.arcfacilities.com/product/equipment/qr-codes-for-equipment-and-assets and https://openqr.uk/solutions/asset-tagging/equipment-manuals

## Stage 3 shortlist

Advance five concepts for deeper work:

1. **W2-G4 — sponsor proof/reporting automation**;
2. **W2-D1 — lightweight RFP requirement/compliance extractor**;
3. **W2-F1 — QR equipment handover pages sold through installers**;
4. **W2-I1 — highly specialized test-data/QA fixture packs**; and
5. **W2-D4 — narrow PO/invoice exception checker**, mainly to determine whether a small file-based product can avoid competing with full AP platforms.

The key Stage 3 question is not whether these problems exist. It is whether a deliberately small product can occupy a useful gap between manual work and large software suites.
