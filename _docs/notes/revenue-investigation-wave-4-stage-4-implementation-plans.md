# Revenue Investigation — Wave 4 Stage 4 Implementation Plans

## Recommendation

Wave 4 adds three credible e-commerce concepts to the broader opportunity inventory:

1. **Amazon SAFE-T evidence/deadline workflow** — strongest Wave 4 candidate.
2. **AI product-answer accuracy monitor** — novel, recurring and highly automatable, but dependent on fast-changing AI surfaces.
3. **DIM-weight packaging savings diagnostic** — simple and financially measurable, but likely more of a diagnostic/service product than a large SaaS business.

No Stage 5 build is authorized.

---

# Candidate W4-1 — Amazon SAFE-T evidence/deadline workflow

## Plain-English offer

“Show us your Amazon seller-fulfilled return/refund records and we organize possible SAFE-T cases, deadlines and evidence so you can review and submit stronger claims on time.”

## Exact first customer

**Small Amazon agencies, prep centres and FBM-focused seller-service firms** that support multiple seller accounts.

Why start with service providers rather than individual sellers:

- one relationship can expose multiple seller accounts/cases;
- they already understand SAFE-T language and workflow;
- they can validate whether the output actually saves time;
- they may become a reseller/referral channel later.

## MVP boundary

The first version should not connect directly to Seller Central or file claims automatically.

It should accept exported/supplied data and create:

- potentially eligible case queue;
- filing deadline;
- missing evidence checklist;
- order/refund/return timeline;
- factual draft claim summary;
- attachment checklist;
- Amazon follow-up response deadline where applicable;
- final “seller review required” status.

## Compliance/platform rule

Amazon's current policy must be treated as source-of-truth. The system must never state that reimbursement is guaranteed or encourage duplicate/ineligible claims.

Every rule should store the policy version/date it was derived from.

## Proposed test pricing — estimate

Two models to test:

- CA$49/month for a low-volume seller/service provider; or
- CA$5–$10 per prepared case during validation.

Partner/reseller pricing could later apply to agencies or prep centres.

## Semi-automated acquisition plan

### Prospect discovery — automated

Build a prospect pool from public Amazon-agency, prep-centre and seller-service directories/search results.

Score for firms that publicly mention:

- Amazon FBM or SFP;
- returns/refunds;
- account management;
- seller operations;
- reimbursement/claims assistance; or
- multiple managed seller accounts.

### Personalization — AI-assisted

Use only public facts from the prospect's site.

Example message angle:

“You manage Amazon seller operations and FBM accounts. We’re testing a small workflow that turns return/refund exports into a SAFE-T deadline and evidence queue, so staff do not have to assemble each case manually.”

### Sample offer

Offer to process **one historical or anonymized SAFE-T case free** and return a structured packet.

Do not require account credentials.

### Outreach workflow

- qualify 40–60 relevant agencies/prep centres;
- manually review first 10 messages;
- send small compliant batches;
- one follow-up during validation;
- route interested replies to a secure upload/intake form;
- suppress opt-outs immediately.

### Reply triage

- wants sample → intake link;
- manages no FBM accounts → close;
- asks for auto-filing → tag as future request, decline in MVP;
- asks about reimbursement guarantee → clarify no guarantee;
- interested in reseller/multi-account → owner notification;
- unsubscribe → suppress.

## Owner burden target

Before positive replies: under **30 minutes/week**.

Pilot review: target under **10 minutes per case** after the first few examples.

## Estimated MVP cost

CA$0–$100 before sales, plus modest AI/document-processing usage if used.

## Go / kill criteria

**Advance:**

- 2 paying service-provider pilots; or
- 5 prospects who submit real/anonymized cases and ask to reuse the workflow.

**Kill/reshape:**

- sellers/service providers say native Amazon tools are already sufficient;
- case preparation still requires substantial manual policy interpretation;
- or most value depends on direct Seller Central automation from day one.

## Main failure mode

This can become a policy-heavy claims service rather than a low-touch product. If expert review is required for most cases, stop or reposition.

# Candidate W4-2 — AI product-answer accuracy monitor

## Plain-English offer

“We regularly check what AI shopping tools say about your products and flag incorrect or unsupported answers before shoppers rely on them.”

## Exact first customer

**Technical-product DTC brands and manufacturers** with 20–500 products and meaningful specifications/compatibility details.

Good first categories:

- outdoor equipment;
- tools/accessories;
- electronics accessories;
- specialty home equipment;
- hobby/technical products.

Avoid fashion/beauty first because many important answers are subjective rather than factual.

## MVP output

For a selected set of products/questions:

- verified merchant fact;
- AI question asked;
- AI answer captured;
- date/surface;
- correct / missing / questionable / incorrect classification;
- exact discrepancy;
- probable source-data issue if obvious;
- recommended fact-page/catalog correction where appropriate.

## Product boundary

This is **accuracy monitoring**, not guaranteed “AI SEO.”

Do not promise ranking improvement, recommendation inclusion or sales lift.

## Proposed test pricing — estimate

- free sample on 3 products;
- CA$49/month small monitoring pack;
- CA$99–$199/month larger catalog/agency tier if validated.

## Semi-automated acquisition plan

### Prospect discovery — automated

Discover DTC/Shopify brands and scan product pages.

Score highly where catalogs have:

- many numerical specifications;
- compatibility requirements;
- technical claims;
- dimensions/capacity/material ratings;
- warranty or usage restrictions;
- products where wrong facts can trigger returns.

### Personalized sample — automated with review

For each qualified brand:

1. select one public product;
2. extract 3–5 verifiable facts from the merchant's site/manual;
3. ask a small approved question set on supported AI surfaces;
4. identify any discrepancy;
5. create a one-page sample.

If no discrepancy exists, do not fabricate urgency. The sample can simply show “all tested answers matched.”

### Outreach message

Short and evidence-led:

“We tested three factual shopper questions about [product]. One AI answer differed from your published specification for [fact]. Here’s the exact comparison. We’re testing a small recurring monitor for this.”

### Acquisition workflow

- identify 50 specification-heavy brands;
- generate samples only where tool/platform terms permit;
- manually review first 10;
- send controlled outreach;
- positive replies go to monitoring intake;
- all routine no/opt-out responses are suppressed automatically.

## Owner burden target

Under **30–45 minutes/week** before positive replies.

If validated, reports should be mostly automated with manual review only for ambiguous discrepancies.

## Estimated MVP cost

CA$25–$150 for API/query usage and hosting during validation, depending on supported AI surfaces.

## Go / kill criteria

**Advance:**

- material factual discrepancies found in at least 20–30% of qualified sample brands; and
- 3 paid pilots or 5 brands asking for recurring monitoring from the first 50 strong prospects.

**Kill/reshape:**

- meaningful errors are rare;
- automated querying is not permitted/reliable enough;
- or buyers care only about visibility/ranking rather than correctness.

## Main failure mode

The market may consolidate around broader AI-search monitoring tools before a narrow accuracy product gains traction. Speed of validation matters more here than for most candidates.

# Candidate W4-3 — DIM-weight packaging savings diagnostic

## Plain-English offer

“Upload your recent orders, package sizes and product dimensions. We show where oversized boxes are increasing your carrier bill and which packaging changes are worth testing.”

## Exact first customer

**Small Shopify/DTC brands shipping roughly hundreds to several thousand parcels per month**, particularly bulky/light products and merchants without WMS/cartonization software.

## MVP output

One report showing:

- actual vs dimensional billable weight where data permits;
- orders most affected;
- box sizes causing the largest estimated excess;
- SKU/order patterns repeatedly using oversized packaging;
- alternative box scenarios;
- estimated monthly/annual savings range;
- caveat where fragility or packing rules need human validation.

## Product boundary

This is not live warehouse cartonization.

It is an **offline diagnostic** using historical order/package data.

No carrier contract negotiation, no WMS integration and no automated pack-station decisions in the first product.

## Proposed test pricing — estimate

- first diagnostic free or CA$49;
- standard diagnostic CA$99–$199;
- optional CA$39–$59/month refresh if merchants want recurring monitoring.

## Semi-automated acquisition plan

### Prospect discovery — automated

Find DTC merchants whose products are likely light relative to box volume.

Score public catalogs for signals such as:

- pillows/home textiles;
- lightweight home goods;
- bundled apparel;
- large accessories;
- pet products;
- multipacks;
- hobby products with air/void space.

Exclude very small stores and obvious enterprise/WMS users.

### Outreach diagnostic offer

Because package data is private, do not pretend to know they have a DIM problem.

Use a diagnostic question:

“We’re testing a simple shipping-cost audit for brands where box size, not product weight, drives carrier charges. If you can export recent order/package dimensions, we return a savings report without connecting to your store.”

### Sample mechanism

Provide a public calculator/demo showing how dimensional weight changes with box dimensions, then offer the merchant-specific diagnostic after interest.

### Reply triage

- wants audit → secure file intake;
- does not track package dimensions → tag as unqualified;
- already uses cartonization/WMS → close;
- asks for live packing recommendations → future request, not MVP;
- interested in recurring audit → owner notification.

## Owner burden target

Prospecting under **30 minutes/week**.

Report QA target under **15 minutes per merchant** once the file format is standardized.

## Estimated MVP cost

CA$0–$75 before sales.

## Go / kill criteria

**Advance:**

- at least 3 merchants provide real data; and
- at least 2 reports identify savings worth 5–10× the proposed audit fee; and
- at least 1–2 merchants pay for a second/expanded diagnostic.

**Kill/reshape:**

- most merchants lack usable package data;
- savings are generally too small;
- or every recommendation needs warehouse-specific manual engineering.

## Main failure mode

The product may be useful but too infrequent to support recurring revenue. If so, it should remain a one-off diagnostic or lead generator rather than SaaS.
