# 300-Idea Second-Chance Review

## Status

The repository contains 20 research files: Stage 1–4 for each of five 60-idea waves. **All selected options have reached Stage 4 where warranted; this does not mean all 300 have received full deep investigation.** No customer validation or Stage 5 product build has occurred. This document starts the systematic reconsideration, not a claim that 300 individual rescue assessments are complete.

## Review method

Every original idea should have one audit row: wave/ID, original proposition, original stage reached, reason it was screened out or retained, evidence strength, proposed modification, changed buyer, changed product boundary, changed distribution, changed pricing, competitor/free alternative, estimated operator burden, and verdict. Original IDs and decisions must remain immutable. Mark unknown evidence explicitly.

Use these failure codes: C = direct competition/free substitution; D = weak discoverability/distribution; W = uncertain willingness to pay; E = unfavorable unit economics; M = manual maintenance/support; P = platform/legal/privacy dependence; T = technical accuracy/feasibility; N = weak/narrow need; R = retained at Stage 4, not rejected.

A rescue is meaningful only when it changes the reason for rejection, not merely the name or marketing language. Evaluate three levers: (1) narrow to an underserved customer or workflow; (2) package a concrete, measurable output rather than a generic feature; (3) move to self-serve/marketplace/inbound distribution and pricing with defensible unit economics. If none helps, keep rejected.

## Deliverables for full audit

1. **300-row inventory** with a disposition for every original idea; do not silently omit the 40 per wave not shortlisted for Stage 2.
2. **Rescue shortlist** ranked by whether the proposed change directly fixes the original failure, low initial cost, no sustained cold outreach, low ongoing effort and plausible paid demand.
3. **Stage 3 re-investigation** of the strongest genuinely revised ideas, checking free alternatives, current competitors, buyer intent and unit economics.
4. **Stage 4 revised plans** for those that survive, including self-serve or compliant low-touch acquisition, explicit price-to-customer-count arithmetic and go/kill tests.
5. **Cross-wave deduplication** so rebranded versions of already retained concepts are not counted as newly discovered businesses.

## Gate

No Stage 5 building, customer outreach, payment setup or launch without explicit user approval. Rejection by desk research must never be described as failure in real-world customer validation.
